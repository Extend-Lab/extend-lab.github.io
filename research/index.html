Research Areas

What We Explore

Multi-Agent Systems

Collaborative and competitive intelligence for complex cyber-physical and physical-world tasks.

Robotic/Embodied AI

Agents that perceive, act, adapt, and learn robustly in uncertain and dynamic environments.

Simulation-to-Reality RL

Bridging virtual training environments and physical deployment through robust reinforcement learning pipelines.

Edge Intelligence and Wireless Systems

Learning-enabled communications, resource allocation, and intelligent infrastructure.
